## What this means

The flywheel loop is proven end-to-end on real evidence: **measure the
failure (gate) → isolate the habit (anatomy) → train exactly that habit
on a corpus the gate never saw → the held-out gate confirms.** A fast,
fully-offloaded 14B now holds mutating verbs on consumer hardware —
the local-AI mission's target shape. The 27B remains the only model to
pass untrained; the 14B is the first to be *made* to pass.

## Caveats

- Per-(model, envelope): the pass is under envelope-v3; v1/v2 configs of
  this model are unmeasured (fail-closed read-only).
- N=20 (perfect-score lower bound 0.839); boots-only; greedy.
- The corpus and gate share the codec-tasks-v1 SHAPE by design; the
  contamination guard proves disjoint content, not disjoint task
  *genre* — generalization beyond single-defect repair tasks is
  unmeasured and out of G4's scope.
- The adapter/GGUF live outside the repo (`~/flywheel1/`); shas above
  are the identity anchors.
